閩中契約的敘事與語言How Minzhong Deeds Speak

The narrative and linguistic structure of the Fuzhou-area land records, read from the team's OCR output.

What was read

The OCR_results folder has 407 HTML pages covering 234 items from the University of Pittsburgh's Chinese Land Records. Each page breaks into labelled layout blocks, mostly Text (5,103 blocks), with 310 Section-Header and 258 Image blocks; the image blocks are model-written descriptions of seals and stamps. The recognised text adds up to about 140,000 characters. I joined each page to the item catalogue in the jackieldyh branch (chinese_land_records_235.csv) by its Pitt ID, which gives a date and a county for every item.

1. The private deed tells one story every time

Whatever the deed type (sale, conditional sale, top-up or final sale), the private contract runs through the same fixed sequence of moves. It is a short first-person story told in the third person. The seller narrates how he came to own the land and why he must part with it. Then he lists, one by one, every claim that could later be raised against the buyer, and shuts each one down.

  1. 立〔賣斷〕契 + nameThe contract's title and its author, in a single phrase: "the one who sets up this sale deed is…". The deed names itself by its legal type.
  2. 承祖 / 承父手置 / 鬮書份下Provenance. The land came from the grandfather, from the father, or from the family division document. Ownership is told as inheritance.
  3. 坐落…土名… / 四至 / 受種 / 年載租Description, located by native place-name (土名). The land is measured by seed sown and rent owed, not by area.
  4. 今因 + motiveThe turning point: "now, because…" (要用, 別置, 無錢應用). The reason is kept formulaic and vague.
  5. 自願託中 … 送與 / 賣與 [line break] X 處為業Voluntariness and mediation. The buyer's name often starts a new line, a spatial form of courtesy (see §3).
  6. 三面言議 … 價銀 … 正Agreement "on three sides" (seller, buyer, middleman). The price is written in formal numerals and closed with 正 ("exactly").
  7. 其銀即日隨契交足Payment is complete at the moment of writing.
  8. 其田即聽 … 管業 / 收租 / 耕作Transfer of use rights: what the buyer may now do.
  9. 亦未曾重張典當…來歷不明…出頭承當，不涉 X 之事Warranty: a chain of negated scenarios, each assigned to the seller.
  10. 面約…年為限…取贖 / 永斷萬無…不得言贖言找Time clause. A conditional sale opens a redemption window; a final sale closes it for good.
  11. 兩相允願，各無反悔。今欲有憑，立…壹紙為照Closing couplet and the act of writing itself: the paper is the proof.
  12. Date · 知契 · 在見 · 中人 · 代筆Signature block: a small public of family, witnesses, middleman and scribe.

2. Deeds are chapters in a longer story

Deeds come in series. The vocabulary of deed types sets out a sequence of stages, and the most common opening phrases in the OCR show it:

StageOpening phrases (pages)What it does
Conditional sale立典契 47 · 立轉典契 4 · 立繳典契 5 · 立胎押字 3 · 立當山契 3The land changes hands with a right to buy it back
Top-up payment立湊字 6 · 立增找杜新契 3The seller gets an extra payment on a deal already made
Final sale立盡根斷賣契 7 · 立湊斷契 6"Exhausts the root": no more claims
Outright sale立賣斷契 45 · 立賣斷根面全田契 19 · 立賣斷皮骨山契 4Permanent transfer, sometimes of both layers of the land

The finality vocabulary escalates: 賣 → 斷 → 盡根斷 → 杜. Final-sale deeds pile up negated verbs to shut the story down: 永斷萬無，嗣後不得言贖言找言盡 ("cut off for ever; hereafter none may speak of redeeming, of topping up, of exhausting"). The parallel 言X言Y construction names, then forbids, the next chapter that the earlier deeds had left open.

The deeds also cite their own predecessors. A third of private pages (33 of 118) record handing over earlier papers (併繳…契壹紙), and 16 count the total (共成…紙). An 1870 sale of a house in Fuzhou by 吳開成 retells three decades of earlier pledges. It then lists 25 papers handed to the buyer: redemption deeds, a loan note, earlier conditional-sale deeds, and 司單執照老契壹拾肆紙 (14 old deeds with tax certificates).

One chain in the corpus can be followed across 117 years. In 嘉慶十年 (1805) 趙守藻 re-pledged a field "with rented-out surface rights" (田面) at 馬坑, 草市都, 侯官縣, for 銀壹拾肆兩, with 楊海武 as middleman. In 1922 the Republican tax office issued a deed-verification certificate (契單) to 林有柱 for a field at 閩侯縣草市都馬坑, "pledged from 趙華藻 in 嘉慶十年", at 原價銀拾肆兩, with 中證楊海武. The place, year, price and middleman all match; 華/守 is very likely an OCR or copying variant. The state form copied the old private deed's story into its own blanks.

3. Linguistic features

Voice and person

Deeds are written in the seller's voice, but the seller usually refers to himself by name (係成與枝等出頭承當). The text reads like testimony recorded on his behalf, which matches the frequent named scribe (代筆, 73 of 118 pages). The buyer is always "X 處" ("the place or house of X"). This is a locative, not a personal name used directly, so the buyer is addressed indirectly as a household.

Courtesy by layout

In many deeds the OCR text breaks the line right after 賣與 / 寄與 / 送與, and the buyer's name starts a new column (托中寄與 ⏎ 兄文鳳處; 賣與 ⏎ 西房平處). This matches the Qing practice of moving an honoured party's name to the top of a new column (抬頭), and it shows up even after the OCR flattened the columns into lines. Official forms do the same for the issuing authority: ⏎財政部核准, 奉 ⏎令.

Four-character rhythm and parallelism

The binding clauses are cast as four-character pairs: 兩相允願，各無反悔; 價足心願，永斷萬無; 高低相配，肥瘦相兼 (from a family division document). The rhythm makes the clauses easy to remember and hard to alter, so a scribe could compose a deed from memory and a reader could spot a deviation.

Negation as the core of the guarantee

The warranty section is built almost entirely from negations: 未曾、並無、不得、無干、不涉、無異. Instead of stating the seller's title in positive terms, the deed lists the ways the title could fail (a prior pledge, a kinsman's claim, a missing tax registration) and denies each one. Ownership is defined by excluding claims, in line with the deed's practical job: being produced when a dispute arises.

Anti-tamper writing

Money and rent are written in formal numerals (壹貳叁…拾佰仟; about 3,200 of them in the corpus) and closed with 正 ("exactly"). Sixteen deeds certify their own corrections: 內改藻字壹字再為憑, 內塗銷合字，又添註憑字. Every change to the text is itself written into the text.

A layered vocabulary of land

The deeds divide the same field into a root or bone layer (根 / 骨), which owes the land tax, and a surface or skin layer (面 / 皮), which carries the right to farm it. The names are 田根 16, 田面 16, 根面全田 24, 皮骨 8. This is Fujian's "one field, two owners" (一田兩主) system written into grammar: a deed must name which layer it transfers. Land is measured by seed sown and rent owed (受種…年載租谷…斤), not by area, until Republican forms add blanks for 畝分厘.

A family audience

The signature block shows who had to approve: mothers and sisters-in-law as 知契…氏 (29 pages), brothers and nephews as co-signers, lineage elders in family division documents and agreements, plus the middleman and scribe. The 1924 agreement of the 林 family has over twenty signers, grouped by branch: 長房、次房、三房、族人、懿戚、公親、族鄰. It shows the deed as a statement made before the whole kin community.

4. The state's voice

Official texts tell a different kind of story: a chain of reported commands. Qing and Republican forms share one bureaucratic framework:

  1. 為…事Purpose heading ("concerning the issuing of a deed certificate")
  2. 案奉 / 奉 ⏎ 令 + quoted regulationThe higher authority's words, quoted inside the document
  3. 等因，奉此"…thus it said; having received this…"
  4. 在案。今據 X 業戶…Back to the case: the applicant's land and the facts of the private deed fill the blanks
  5. 合行填給…須至…者The performative act of issuing
  6. 右給業戶 X 准此Address and closing

Three changes stand out:

  • RepublicThe state starts arguing about evidence. Deed-verification certificates warn that an unverified deed 於訴訟時不能作為憑據 (cannot serve as evidence in a lawsuit). Forms print sliding scales of fines for under-reported prices. The private deed's warranty against third-party claims is matched by a state threat of making the deed void.
  • 1953The narrative gives way to the form. The People's Government deed paper (福建省人民政府公定分拆契紙) has labelled fields only: 承受人姓名, 原所有人姓名, 不動產種類, 坐落, 面積, 價, 中證人, 監證人. None of the bureaucratic storytelling formulae survive. The middleman sits next to a new "supervising witness" (監證人). Dates are written as 公元一九五三年, and simplified characters appear in some 1950s–60s items.
  • Hybrid sheetsOn 35 pages a private contract and a state certificate share one sheet. The OCR interleaves the two voices: the seller's 今欲有憑 beside the province's 合填驗契執照. The state literally attaches itself to the private story (粘連原契).
